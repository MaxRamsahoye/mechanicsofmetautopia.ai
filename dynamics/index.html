Cultural Dynamics Simulator

A cellular automaton for topologically modelling civilisation-scale cultural dynamics across sovereign political units, for The Mechanics of Meta-Utopia. A culture spreads to a neighbouring sub-topia when its cultural integrity overcomes both that sub-topia’s membrane impermeability and its own cultural integrity.
