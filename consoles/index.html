Consoles

Interactive consoles for The Mechanics of Meta-Utopia. The first two open on their own sites.

  1. Console 1

    Civilisational Design Modifier

    A civilisation designer console: steer a civilisation through the four post-AGI cultural attractor states, by cultural integrity and membrane permeability.

    Open the console ↗

  2. Console 2

    Cultural Dynamics Simulator

    A simulator of cultural dynamics: watch cultures form, spread, migrate and converge or fragment over time.

    Open the console ↗

  3. Console 3

    Phase Portrait

    The four attractor states as a flow field: arrows, basins of attraction and civilisations drifting into them, reshaped by dials for AI capabilities.

    Open the console →

  4. Console 4

    Membranes

    Sub-topias as cells with gapped membranes: people and memes cross, mix, fade or flood as cultural integrity and membrane permeability change.

    Open the console →
