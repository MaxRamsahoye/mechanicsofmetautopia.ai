How it works

Seven sub-topias are drawn as cells. Their membranes have six gaps, as wide as the membrane’s permeability; particles pass through a gap and bounce off the membrane. Each particle is a person or a meme, and carries its culture as a colour.

  • Cultural integrity. A strong cell recolours the particles inside it with its own culture and keeps its culture whatever arrives; a weak one takes on the culture of whatever is inside it, and its colours fade to grey.
  • Membrane permeability. The wider the gaps, the more particles cross between cells, carrying their culture with them.
  • Contact. Particles that meet trade some of their colour, the fitter culture more strongly. One culture is expansionist: fitter, and firm in its own cell.

Sealed membranes around strong cells give enclosure; open membranes around strong cells give uplift, with exchange and every culture kept. Open membranes around weak cells let the expansionist culture flood the map (takeover); closed membranes around weak cells leave cultures to fade (collapse). In between, the clash. The same four futures, with a full description of each, are in the typology of the four attractor states.
