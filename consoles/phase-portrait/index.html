How to read it

The plane is the article’s 2×2 of the four attractor states: membrane permeability across, cultural integrity up. Each state is a well, and the clash in the middle is a spiral, cultures circling one another without settling. A civilisation anywhere in the plane drifts downhill into one of them, and the shaded region around each well is its basin of attraction: every civilisation that starts there ends there.

  • Offence–defence balance. Defence-dominant technology (shields: borders, filters, isolation) deepens the well of enclosure; offence-dominant technology (spears: persuasion, propaganda, memetic replicators) deepens the well of takeover.
  • Coordination capacity. Epistemic and coordination technology (a moral parliament of AI representatives, guardian angels, a world digital democracy) deepens the well of uplift; without it, collapse and the clash deepen.
  • Shocks. Wars, pandemics and breakthroughs shake civilisations, so that they can climb out of a shallow well into another.

The bars read off the odds: how much of the space drains into each state. Moving the dials redraws the basins, which is the argument of the article in miniature: the technologies built in the transition to AGI decide not only where a civilisation is, but where it can end up.
