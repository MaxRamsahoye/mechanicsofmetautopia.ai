Civilisation Designer Console

The console of a civilisational designer with control over two critical variables: the intra-cultural self-reproduction (cultural integrity) and inter-cultural self-protection (membrane permeability) of sub-topias. Set the two dials and watch the civilisation’s map move into one of the four post-AGI cultural attractor states of The Mechanics of Meta-Utopia, or the Clash of Civilisations between them. Initially developed as a separate site.
